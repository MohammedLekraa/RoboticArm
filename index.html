01. System Architecture & Data Flow

The system captures spatial orientation and finger kinematics via non-invasive sensory glove hardware. High-rate analog voltages and I2C orientation vectors are converted into structured telemetry packets and transmitted over a connectionless, low-overhead wireless protocol.

02. Electrical Engineering & Power Management

Actuator high-current demand required dedicated power rail separation to prevent voltage sag induced microcontroller brownout resets.

  • Power Rail Isolation: Independent 5V/6A DC-DC Buck converter for servomotors and regulated 3.3V LDO for MCU/Sensors.
  • Signal Filtering: RC low-pass passive filtering ($f_c \approx 50\text{Hz}$) on raw flex sensor analog outputs to attenuate high-frequency noise.
  • Decoupling Matrix: Bulk electrolytic capacitors ($1000\mu\text{F}$) on high-current bus paired with ceramic capacitors ($0.1\mu\text{F}$) near IC power pins.
