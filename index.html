01. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW

The system captures spatial orientation and finger kinematics via non-invasive sensory glove hardware. High-rate analog voltages and $\text{I}^2\text{C}$ orientation vectors are converted into structured telemetry packets and transmitted over a connectionless, low-overhead peer-to-peer wireless protocol.

02. ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING & POWER MANAGEMENT

Actuator high-current demand required dedicated power rail separation to prevent voltage sag induced microcontroller brownout resets.

  • Power Rail Isolation: Independent 5V/6A DC-DC Buck converter for servomotors and a regulated 3.3V LDO for MCU/Sensors.
  • Signal Filtering: RC low-pass passive filtering ($f_c \approx 50\text{Hz}$) on raw flex sensor analog outputs to attenuate high-frequency noise from mechanical vibration.
  • Decoupling Matrix: Bulk electrolytic capacitors ($1000\mu\text{F}$) on the high-current bus paired with ceramic capacitors ($0.1\mu\text{F}$) near IC power pins.

03. KINEMATICS & SENSOR CALIBRATION

Raw ADC readings from flex resistors exhibit non-linear behavior. Non-linear mapping algorithms dynamically remap digital values to servo angular degrees ($0^\circ - 180^\circ$) using calibrated threshold bounds:

Angle = map(ADC_raw, ADC_min, ADC_max, 0, 180)

For wrist movement tracking, roll and pitch are extracted from the MPU6050 accelerometer and gyroscope data using a Complementary Filter to prevent gyroscopic drift over time:

Angle_{total} = 0.98 \times (Angle + \omega \cdot \Delta t) + 0.02 \times Angle_{accel}
